## The programme

`python3 scripts/modularity_queue.py [--extract|--compose|--json]`

258 files exceed 200 lines: **214 extractable**, 44 needing composition.
This is much larger than the 136 under `src/` because the `datasets/`

Strategy per file is decided mechanically by `self.` references:

- **extract** (self <= 2): independent helpers, safe to move into a
  package. 214 files.
- **compose** (self > 2): object behaviour. Moving methods breaks them.
  44 files, each its own change.
